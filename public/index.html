The postscript

Before the first letter arrives

What is Ellestia Mail Club?

Ellestia Mail Club is the brand behind The Elsewhere Post, a monthly physical mail club exploring one extraordinary destination through a letter and illustrated paper keepsakes.

How much does a monthly edition cost?

The planned price is ₹499 per month, including domestic shipping within India.

Where do you ship?

We currently offer domestic shipping within India. International shipping may be available in the future.

When will my mail arrive?

Subscriptions for the January edition are open from 1 December to 20 December. Letters will be dispatched on 30 December, and you can expect your mail to arrive between 7 and 10 January.

How do payments and renewals work?

Each edition is a separate one-time payment of ₹499. There is no automatic renewal; you choose whether to purchase each monthly edition.

Can I cancel?

You can cancel anytime. Each month is a one-time payment, so you are not locked into a long-term commitment.
