What is Ellestia Mail Club?

The Elsewhere Post is a physical mail club exploring one extraordinary destination through paper keepsakes, stories and activities.

What is included?

Each edition includes a destination letter, an art print, bookmark, activity and themed stickers.

How much does it cost?

₹499, including domestic shipping within India.

When will the January edition ship?

Orders are open December 1–20, 2026. The January edition is scheduled to dispatch December 30, 2026.

Is this an automatically renewing subscription?

No. Each edition is a one-time purchase. You decide whether to order again.

How long does delivery take?

Delivery typically takes around 7 business days after dispatch, depending on the postal service and location.
