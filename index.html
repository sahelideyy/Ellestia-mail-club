The postscript

Before the first letter arrives

What is Ellestia Mail Club?

Ellestia Mail Club is a monthly mystery-by-mail experience. Each edition of The Elsewhere Post takes you to a real place through stories, curious facts, fictional mysteries, games and puzzles. Explore through the pages of your newspaper, uncover clues, solve little challenges and find the answers in your accompanying answer sheet. It’s a little travel, a little storytelling and a little mystery, delivered to your door.

How much does a monthly edition cost?

Each monthly edition costs ₹499, with domestic shipping within India included.

Where do you ship?

We currently ship within India.

When will my mail arrive?

Subscriptions for the January edition are open from 1 December to 20 December. All orders will be dispatched on 30 December, and your mail should arrive within 7 business days of dispatch.

How do payments and renewals work?

Each edition is purchased individually. Your payment covers that edition and is not an automatic recurring charge.

Can I cancel?

Each edition is purchased individually, so there is no recurring payment to cancel. If you have already placed an order, please refer to our order and cancellation policy.

Do I need to purchase previous editions to participate?

Not at all! Every edition is a brand-new experience with its own destination, fictional mystery, stories, games and puzzles. You can join with any edition you like. There is no storyline you need to follow from the beginning.

Are the mysteries real?

The destinations, places, history and facts featured in our editions are rooted in real life. The mysteries, characters and investigative scenarios are fictional, created especially for each edition.
